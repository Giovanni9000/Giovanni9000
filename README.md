### About Me

I'm a Full-Stack Developer based in **South Africa**, trained at ZAIO on the full MERN stack. I build responsive frontends, RESTful APIs, and complete web applications with React, Node.js, Express, MongoDB and Firebase.

Before pivoting into tech I spent 3+ years in financial advisory, logistics coordination and agricultural operations across South Africa and Ghana. That background gives me something most junior developers don't have — I understand what real business problems look like before they become tickets, and I can communicate clearly with non-technical stakeholders.

- 🔭 Currently building AI automation workflows (n8n + LLMs) and strengthening backend skills (Node.js, JWT, Redis)
- 🌱 Actively looking for junior full-stack developer roles
- 💬 Ask me about React, Node.js, Firebase, MongoDB or turning business processes into software
- ⚡ Fun fact: I went from farm work and financial planning straight into shipping full-stack projects
